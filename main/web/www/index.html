      Probed separately from the ping, so "the internet is down" and "DNS is down" can be told apart. Answers may be served from the resolver cache for their TTL.

      Secret vault

       

      At least 8 characters. The key is derived from this every time and is never stored, so losing it means losing the secrets — there is no reset that keeps them.

      What this does and does not do: a locked vault keeps credentials out of the web UI and out of the device export, and a flash dump yields only PBKDF2-hardened ciphertext. It does not protect against someone watching LAN traffic while the vault is unlocked — the dashboard is served over plain HTTP, so a revealed secret crosses the network in the clear.
